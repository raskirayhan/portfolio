# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be useful, welcoming, and technically honest. Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and open to feedback.

## Expected behavior

- Use inclusive and professional language.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and curiosity.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, sexualized language, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to `raskirayhan@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed and handled in a way that protects the safety and privacy of everyone involved.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
